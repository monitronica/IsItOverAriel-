# Press and References

This bibliography documents *Je že konec? / Is It Over?* and separates official production records, independent criticism, creator documentation, and event or award notices. Links were checked on 9 November 2026. Some sources are in Slovenian.

## Official production pages

### Moment — *Is it over?*

- **Publisher:** Moment
- **URL:** https://moment.si/en/performance/is-it-over/
- **Description:** Official English production page with synopsis, full credits, photographer attribution, festival information, performance dates, gallery and trailer.

### Maribor Puppet Theatre — *Is it over yet?*

- **Publisher:** Maribor Puppet Theatre
- **URL:** https://www.lg-mb.si/en/past-seasons/is-it-over-yet/
- **Description:** Official English archive entry from the co-producer, including the artistic framing, cast and crew, gallery, and November 2022 premiere information.

### Maribor Puppet Theatre — *Je že konec?*

- **Publisher:** Lutkovno gledališče Maribor
- **URL:** https://www.lg-mb.si/predstave/je-ze-konec/
- **Description:** Current Slovenian production record. It lists the 45-minute duration, credits, responses, Zlata paličica recommendation, and 2023 ZIZ award.

### SiGledal — *Je že konec?*

- **Publisher:** SiGledal / Slovenian theatre portal
- **URL:** https://veza.sigledal.org/uprizoritev/je-ze-konec
- **Description:** Archival production record for the 2022/23 season, documenting the 25 November 2022 premiere at Intimni oder GT22, the creative team, performers, duration and festival appearance.

### Zlata paličica — *Je že konec?*

- **Publisher:** Zlata paličica / Slovenian Theatre Institute
- **URL:** https://www.zlatapalicica.si/predstava/je-ze-konec/
- **Description:** Expert recommendation for the experimental performance, with production data and a committee assessment by Nika Arhar, Martina Peštaj and Blanka Gombač. It records a 45-minute duration, a recommended audience of 14–18, and the premiere date.

## Press reviews

These are critical responses rather than producer announcements or event listings.

### “(GLEDALIŠKA KRITIKA) Don't stop me now*”

- **Author:** Petra Vidali
- **Publication:** *Večer*
- **Date:** 5 December 2022
- **URL:** https://vecer.com/kultura/gledaliska-kritika-dont-stop-me-now-10322413
- **Description:** A review of the premiere production and its 1 December performance. Vidali discusses the mechanical voice, surveillance-like eyes, end-of-the-world framing, three performers and the work's effectiveness as a theatrical lesson for young audiences.

### “Konec sveta” (“The End of the World”)

- **Author:** Tjaša Bertoncelj
- **Publication:** SiGledal
- **Date:** Not displayed in the accessible record
- **URL:** https://veza.sigledal.org/kritika/konec-sveta-r
- **Description:** A Slovenian-language critical response to *Je že konec?*. The official Maribor Puppet Theatre page identifies the title, author and publication and links directly to this review.

## Creator and project documentation

### Andrej Firm — *Is it Over?*

- **Author / publisher:** Andrej Firm
- **Date:** 30 November 2022
- **URL:** https://www.andrejfirm.com/is-it-over/
- **Description:** Project page by the production's lighting and space designer, with the English synopsis, full team credits, premiere month and links to the co-producers.

### *Je že konec?* educational guide

- **Prepared by:** Maša Radi Buh
- **Publishers:** Moment and Maribor Puppet Theatre
- **URL:** https://moment.si/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Je-ze-konec_KUV_pedagosko_gradivo.pdf
- **Description:** Slovenian educational material describing the devised process, audience interaction, object theatre, DIY scenographic aesthetic, technology and robotics themes, and the production team's practices.

### Moment cultural-education overview

- **Publisher:** Moment
- **URL:** https://moment.si/kuv/
- **Description:** Places the performance in Moment's programme for young audiences and identifies its themes as technology and robotics, ethics, interpersonal relationships, trust, the future, fear, devised theatre and improvisation.

### *Je že konec?* trailer

- **Publisher:** Moment
- **Platform:** Vimeo
- **URL:** https://vimeo.com/786279788
- **Description:** Official production trailer identifying Moment and Maribor Puppet Theatre as co-producers.

## Awards, festivals and institutional records

These sources document reception and circulation but are not independent reviews.

- **Publication:** SiGledal, “Nagrade in nagrajenci sezone 2023/2024”

- **Description:** Season awards roundup recording that *Je že konec?* received the award for performance at the 2023 ZIZ Festival of Maribor alternative theatre production.
